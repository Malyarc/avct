Principles

Bilingual, never doubled

The app speaks English. Chinese appears where the official form does — option labels, section names, the document itself.

One question at a time

The paper form asks 200 things on eight dense pages. Here they arrive in nine calm groups, each with a reason to exist.

The document is the truth

What you preview, print and download is one renderer. Preview and PDF cannot drift apart.
