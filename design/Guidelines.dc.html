Advanced Certification Training for TCCA (Tzu Ching) Alumni

Standards for alumni recommended for Certified Training as a Committee Member or Faith Corps member, for the 2026–2027 cohort.

AVCT Eligibility

TCCA (Tzu Ching) alumni recommended for Certified Training as Committee Member or Faith Corps must meet all of the following requirements:

1

Served as a TCCA (Tzu Ching) officer or cadre and was issued the TCCA uniform while participating in a TCCA club at a U.S. university.

2

Participated as a student or as staff in a national or overseas TCCA Retreat or Conference at least two (2) times.

3

As a TCCA alumnus or alumna after graduation, served at least once as staff for a chapter, national, or overseas TCCA Retreat or Conference.

Flexibility for the Headquarters Region

Recommendations are generally based on meeting all three conditions above. In view of the different environments for TCCA recruitment and activities across chapters, and with input from the Chapter CEO, the Chapter CEO and team retain flexibility to make further adjustments. The Headquarters Region has adjusted the requirement so that meeting any two of the three conditions is sufficient.

Registration

1

Complete the registration form.

2

Upload your 600-word (or more) autobiography.

3

Upload an electronic headshot of you wearing the Tzu Chi grey shirt with white collar, for use in producing the Training ID card.

4

Training attire: grey shirt with white collar, white trousers, Tzu Chi blue belt, white shoes and white socks. Sisters should wear their hair in the Tzu Chi bun or TCCA alumni braids.

Certification Eligibility

1

Complete at least 80% of the AVCT courses (6 sessions in English). In-person attendance at the Closing Ceremony is mandatory.

2

Actively engage in the Tzu Chi Four Missions and Eight Footprints (四大八法); complete at least 300 hours of volunteer service.

3

Report your volunteer hours to your designated Concerted Effort Team Leader by the end of each month.

4

Cultivate at least 20 donor households (not including yourself) through fundraising. Redeem your fundraising record book from the Finance Department at your local region.

5

Complete the Training Handbook.

6

Hold Right Understanding and Right View (正知正見), and fully embody the Tzu Chi spirit and philosophy.
